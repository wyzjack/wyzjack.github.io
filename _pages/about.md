I am a Research Scientist/Engineer on the Applied Research team at [Adobe Photoshop](https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop.html). I work on language and vision-language models that reason carefully, and on turning that reasoning into precise image edits.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu/), advised by Prof. [Yun Raymond Fu](http://www1.ece.neu.edu/~yunfu/).

I want to build reasoning systems that stay truthful when they are uncertain, and that can be trusted as the tasks get harder.

---

## Research

**AI safety.** Models should abstain or answer cautiously when the evidence is weak, and visual models should not invent what is not in the image. [Cautious Next Token Prediction](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2507.03038) (ACL 2025) studies cautious decoding. [Distorted or Fabricated?](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.12944) (ACL 2026) surveys hallucination in video LLMs. [VaQuitA](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.02310.pdf) studies alignment for LLM-assisted video understanding.

**LLM and VLM reasoning.** I work on how multimodal models plan, keep context, and ground intermediate steps in the image. [Efficient Reasoning with Hidden Thinking](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.19201) (ICML 2026), [Revealing the Seen, Imagining the Beyond](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.2087.pdf) (ACL 2026), and [Hierarchical Visual Agent](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.04304) (ACL 2026).

**Reasoning-based image editing.** Instruction-guided editing should understand an object and the effects it causes, not only the pixels of the object. That line of work shipped as Photoshop's [Remove objects and related elements](https://x.com/yizhoujackwang/status/2121140899136889308). Related research includes [From Words to Pixels](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.2155.pdf) (ACL 2026), a survey of LLMs for visual segmentation, and [UniSER](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.14183) (CVPR 2026) on unified soft-effects removal.
